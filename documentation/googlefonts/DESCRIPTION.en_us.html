Gothic Gumdrop is a cute, bubbly blackletter font. Its bold blend of traditional blackletter with modern charm makes it suited to headlines, display use, or metal band posters that need a softer side. It preserves the thickness and ornateness of blackletter while expressing an endearing, mellow side in each letter.

The initial design and glyph set were generated by the Mixfont artificial intelligence model, developed by Eric Lu, from the prompt “a cute, bubbly blackletter font”. For this release, Mixfont partnered with type designer Alex Kaczun of Type Innovations to refine the letterforms, alignments, kerning, and other details.

Designed by Eric Lu (Mixfont) and Alex Kaczun (Type Innovations). To contribute, see the Gothic Gumdrop repository.
